\section{Tom tat do an}

Do an xay dung mot tro ly ao bao mat co kha nang nhan lenh bang giong noi,
nhan dien nguoi noi, xac minh nguoi noi truoc cac tac vu nhay cam, va ca nhan
hoa phan hoi theo tung nguoi dung. He thong duoc chia thanh hai yeu cau chinh:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{YC1 - Speaker Recognition}: train va danh gia mo hinh ECAPA-TDNN
  cho Speaker Identification (SID) va Speaker Verification (SV).
  \item \textbf{YC2 - Secure Virtual Assistant}: tich hop SID/SV vao tro ly ao,
  phan quyen tac vu theo ba muc NORMAL, PERSONAL va IMPORTANT.
\end{itemize}

Ban hien tai da co pipeline end-to-end: thu am tren trinh duyet, decode audio,
VAD, ASR, speaker embedding, NLU, auth gate, intent handler va TTS. Evidence
speaker recognition da duoc tao tren VoxCeleb Indian subset va VIVOS. Checkpoint
VIVOS duoc chon lam checkpoint chinh cho demo tieng Viet vi cho ket qua tot hon
trong thiet lap noi bo.

\section{Speaker Recognition}

\subsection{ECAPA-TDNN}

Mo hinh speaker chinh la ECAPA-TDNN. Cau hinh:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Feature: 80-dim log Mel filterbank.
  \item Normalization: cepstral mean normalization theo truc thoi gian.
  \item Embedding dimension: 192.
  \item Loss: Additive Angular Margin Softmax.
  \item Margin: 0.2.
  \item Scale: 30.
  \item Crop train: 3 giay.
  \item Verification score: cosine similarity giua hai embedding da L2-normalize.
\end{itemize}

Mo hinh duoc train theo bai toan speaker classification. Sau train, phan encoder
duoc tai su dung cho speaker verification bang cosine similarity.

\section{Augmentation va fine-tune noisy}

Codebase da co khung augmentation:

\begin{itemize}
  \item MUSAN noise injection.
  \item RIR reverb.
  \item Speed perturbation 0.9x, 1.0x, 1.1x.
  \item SpecAugment tren log-mel feature.
\end{itemize}

Trong ban nop nay, fine-tune noisy/reverb chua duoc dua vao metric chinh do
gioi han thoi gian va GPU session. Dieu nay khong lam hong do an vi yeu cau cot
loi da co:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Co mo hinh speaker recognition train/evaluate that.
  \item Co checkpoint VIVOS phu hop tieng Viet.
  \item Co SID/SV metrics va artifact.
  \item Co ung dung assistant tich hop speaker gate.
\end{itemize}

Fine-tune noisy nen duoc trinh bay la huong mo rong:

\begin{quote}
He thong hien dung checkpoint ECAPA-TDNN train tren VIVOS. Pipeline fine-tune
voi MUSAN/RIR va du lieu nhieu/re da duoc chuan bi trong code, nhung chua dua
vao bang metric chinh do gioi han thoi gian/GPU. Day la buoc tiep theo de tang
robustness trong moi truong that.
\end{quote}

\section{Danh gia viec chua fine-tune noisy}

Neu khong kip fine-tune noisy/re, anh huong chinh la robustness trong moi truong
co nhieu va re chua duoc chung minh bang so lieu. Tuy nhien, do an van dat phan
cot loi:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Da train/evaluate speaker model tren dataset that.
  \item Da co checkpoint VIVOS tot cho tieng Viet.
  \item Da tich hop SID/SV vao tro ly ao co phan quyen.
  \item Da co bang metric SID, SV, EER, minDCF va threshold.
  \item Da co caveat ro rang ve domain, split va calibration.
\end{itemize}

Khi bao ve, nen noi ngan gon:

\begin{quote}
Phan fine-tune noisy/reverb la huong mo rong da chuan bi pipeline, nhung chua
dua vao ket qua chinh vi gioi han thoi gian train. Ket qua chinh hien tai tap
trung vao checkpoint VIVOS va speaker-gated assistant.
\end{quote}
